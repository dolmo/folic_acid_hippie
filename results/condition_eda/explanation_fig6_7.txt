  Fig 7 — Heatmap: z-scored unit trajectory per condition × DIV

  What it is: 10 heatmaps (one per unit metric), rows = 4 FA conditions, columns = DIV. The z-score here is within-condition: each condition is z-scored relative to its own mean

  What to look for:
  - If all conditions show the same red→blue (or blue→red) gradient across columns, they mature in sync.
  - Half-width: 0mg starts very high (dark red at DIV 3) and drops sharply — its trajectory is more extreme than others. This is a condition difference in the shape of
  development, not just level.
  - Firing range: 0mg again shows a distinctive early-high, late-low trajectory compared to others whose z-score pattern is more uniform.
  - Most metrics show broadly similar patterns across conditions (parallel trajectories), consistent with the Δ test in fig5 — but a few metrics show that 0mg's trajectory is
